      Tips

      • GPS works without internet — keep location turned on.
      • Tap 🧭 to turn on the compass, then follow the arrow next to each place.
      • Map tiles you look at while online are kept for offline viewing. Browse your area once on the Map tab.
      • Many pan shops and tea stalls aren't on the map yet — tap ＋ to add one you find. It's saved only on your phone.
