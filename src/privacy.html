Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Nearby Offline helps you find shops and services near you, and works without internet. It has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking.

Location

The app uses your device's location (GPS) to sort places by distance and to point you toward them. Your location is processed only on your device. It is not sent to us. We don't run any servers that collect data.

Data sent to third-party services

Some data goes to these public OpenStreetMap services, but only when you are online and use these features:

  • Saving an area: the centre coordinates and radius you choose go to an Overpass API server (overpass-api.de, overpass.kumi.systems or overpass.private.coffee) to download the place list.
  • Town search and area names: your search text, or the coordinates of the area you save, go to nominatim.openstreetmap.org.
  • Map view: map data for the part of the map you look at is loaded from tiles.openfreemap.org (OpenFreeMap).

These services receive your IP address as part of any internet request. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy and OpenFreeMap privacy policy.

Data stored on your device

Saved areas, places you add, your last known location and settings are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded. You can delete them at any time from Offline areas, by clearing the app's data, or by uninstalling the app. Export creates a file that you control and choose where to share.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this page. Questions: open an issue on the project page.
